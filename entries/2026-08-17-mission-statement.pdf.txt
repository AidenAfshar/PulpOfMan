We are in an age with an excess of information and a Supreme lack of order, or clear direction for the lives of individuals. Perceptions of reality have been muddled with notions  of subjectivity, objectivity, and some nebulous idea of an absolute truth. We have lost, order, not because of a lack of touch with truth, but a deficiency in the ability to construct a coherent narrative - to mould the story of our lives.

I seek to provide order to my generation through the proliferation of my philosophy and by supplying my own narratives for others to glean wisdom and experience from. That is what you will find in this blog.
