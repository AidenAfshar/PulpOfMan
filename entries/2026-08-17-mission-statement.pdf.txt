We are in an age with an excess of information and a Supreme lack of order. Perceptions of reality have been muddled with notions  of subjectivity, objectivity, and some nebulous idea of an absolute truth. We have lost, order, not because of a lack of touch with truth, but a deficiency in the ability to construct a coherent narrative - the story of our lives.

Just to clarify, by "order," I mean clear
direction for the lives of individuals.

I seek to provide order to my generation
through the proliferation of my philosophy
and by supplying my own narratives
for others to glean, wisdom and
experience, from. That is what you will
find in this blog.
